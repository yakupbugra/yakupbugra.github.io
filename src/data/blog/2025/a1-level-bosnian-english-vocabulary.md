Therefore, I decided to create my own vocabulary list using the English A1 word list from the [English Profile website](https://englishprofile.org/?menu=evp-online&dict=us).

Below, you can find vocabulary lists with **Bosnian-English** translations. Each word and its definition are separated by a *tab* character.
